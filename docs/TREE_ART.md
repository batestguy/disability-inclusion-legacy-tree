## Why this reference
The State House appointment biography and the office About page do not establish an exact hometown. TheCable's 31 May 2025 interview identifies Isa's early JONAPWD Yobe chairmanship, and January 2024 local reporting describes his Damaturu residence; neither proves a birthplace or childhood hometown. Broad name/biography, office, alternate-name, hometown/town and regional tree searches did not establish a more precise link.

The owner explicitly authorised the best nostalgic regional approximation when a precise hometown tree could not be found. A large, weathered Yobe baobab offers a grounded regional choice. Its familiar Hausa name, kuka, is documented in the UCLA African Languages Bole materials: https://aflang.humanities.ucla.edu/language-materials/chadic-languages/yobe/bole/ . Nostalgia is an artistic intention, not a verified biographical fact.

The tree's location comes from the photographer's description; it has not been independently geolocated. **Hon. Isa's exact hometown and personal connection to this particular tree remain unverified.** The public caption states that limit. The source uploader also makes an age claim; no age is repeated because it was not independently substantiated.

Alternatives examined: the Tree environment.jpg Commons photo is a landscape rather than an isolated large tree; Mamudo mango photos were available but less distinctive. Kushi Gando at Daniski was identified as a heritage candidate, but reuse rights for the blog photographs were not established. No unlicensed candidate image was adapted or published.

## Changes and method
Built-in imagegen, transparent background enabled, using the downloaded licensed photograph as the edit reference. Preserved the distinctive pale, stout forked trunk and irregular branch architecture. Restored muted natural colour from sepia, removed background/farm vegetation, artistically completed obscured/cropped crown and base, and recomposed for the landscape tree interface. A second edit adjusted framing. Reconstructed details are artistic; the result is not an unaltered documentary photograph.

Full prompts and selected paths: [tree-art-generation.json](tree-art-generation.json).

- Original licensed photo: output/references/yobe-baobab-original.jpg
- Original licence metadata and source-page wikitext snapshots: output/references/yobe-baobab-metadata.json and yobe-baobab-source-page.json
- Selected AI adaptation: output/imagegen/tree-yobe-adapted.png
- Served derivatives: apps/web/public/tree-yobe.webp and tree-yobe-mobile.webp
- Structured provenance and source SHA-256: content/tree-art.json
- Public licence/change notice: apps/web/public/tree-art-license.txt

Sharp resizing/WebP compression preserves the generated alpha. Previous generic artwork remains archived, but is no longer referenced by the displayed tree.

## Flag watermark
One decorative CSS green-white-green flag spans the entire fixed viewport on home and permanent pages and remains in place while scrolling. It is subtle, pointer-transparent and hidden from assistive technology. High-contrast/forced-colour modes hide it for reading comfort. The earlier local Nigeria-outline watermark was removed from the displayed composition; its Natural Earth asset and source record are retained as historical material.
