# Existing OSSAP-SNEO site review

Reviewed 22 October 2026 at the owner's request. Research and recommendation only: no product pivot approved or implemented.

Subsequent decision: the owner accepted descriptive contribution KPIs and a data-led redesign. That later direction supersedes the decision status above; see CONTRIBUTION_METRICS.md and HANDOFF.md. The findings below remain the historical comparison, not the current implementation plan.

## Finding

Substantial editorial overlap exists. The office-presenting site already provides biography, programme summaries, event photographs, a long news archive and an anniversary account. The legacy tree is a different interface, but an interface alone does not establish a separate need. Our five-record preview is much narrower than that archive.

## Reviewed pages

- [Gallery](https://ossaposneo.org/gallery/): event collections and photographs; some repeated headings. Browser snapshot showed image links pointing to media files. Original HTML contained many empty image alt attributes. This is an accessibility concern for meaningful photographs, not a completed WCAG audit. No branch/year exploration or language selector was found on the reviewed page.
- [Home](https://ossaposneo.org/): office identity and current updates.
- [About](https://ossaposneo.org/about-us/): institutional mission and values.
- [Programmes](https://ossaposneo.org/our-programs-and-initiatives/): education, healthcare, employment and financial support summaries. Two sampled programme links returned to this page.
- [Citation](https://ossaposneo.org/citation-one-year-exploits/): education, appointment and mandate. Exact hometown not established by this page.
- [Anniversary account](https://ossaposneo.org/one-year-in-office-ssap-abba-isa-showcases-remarkable-catalogue-of-extraordinary-achievements/): 13 thematic areas; includes intended future results alongside reported activity. Treat as an office account, not independent impact verification.
- [News archive](https://ossaposneo.org/blog/): substantial dated archive, including political mobilisation and congratulations as well as disability work. Permanent articles and search already exist; these are not unique advantages of our prototype.
- [SMEDAN report](https://ossaposneo.org/ssap-hon-abba-isa-facilitates-empowerment-program-for-pwds-in-fulfillment-of-renewed-hope-agenda/): overlaps our business-skills story. Another publisher repeating an office statement does not automatically provide independent corroboration.
- [SABI-Woman report](https://ossaposneo.org/presidential-aide-abba-isa-outlines-sustainability-roadmap-for-sightsavers-sabi-woman-project/): distinguishes partner implementation from Isa's advocacy contribution. Useful model for shared credit; outcome claims still need original partner verification.

## Comparison with the checked-in application

Existing prototype: five records, four branches, search/year filtering, English and Hausa routes, source types, role/status/evidence-limit fields, permanent links and accessibility preferences. Hausa remains unapproved, portrait absent and performance/human checks incomplete. No multi-person archive or original testimony collection exists. Neither unique verified outcomes nor approved bilingual access should be claimed as delivered advantages yet.

No interactive legacy tree, reviewed Hausa collection or structured cross-source evidence cards were found in the inspected public pages. This is a bounded observation, not proof that no such resource exists anywhere on the domain. No full accessibility, security or performance audit was performed. Desktop browser inspection and source reading were used; mobile interaction was not assessed.

## Recommendation

Keep the code and tree artwork. Avoid building another news/gallery portal. Reframe the intended value around the owner's broader appreciation purpose: carefully credited contributions by people and organisations supporting disability inclusion, with Hon. Isa as the first tribute. A future wider collection is a proposed direction, not an existing feature or an approved scope change.

Before expanding implementation, curate three strong chapters that each answer: what happened, who delivered it, what Isa contributed, what changed, and what the evidence does not establish. Add original, permissioned reflections from collaborators or participants only with explicit consent and contact authorisation; do not contact anyone automatically. Link to the office archive for continuing news. Make bilingual review and accessible reading substantive benefits rather than relying on visual novelty.

Photographs found on the office site are potential permission leads, not cleared assets. Its copyright notice does not grant adaptation/republication rights. No images were imported into public assets. The Yobe tree's personal hometown connection remains unverified; the reviewed citation page does not resolve that.
