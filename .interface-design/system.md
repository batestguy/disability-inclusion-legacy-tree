## System

- Colours: white #fff; achievement green #156146; planned green #156146; ink
  #183e2e; muted #52665b; achievement surface #f4f8f5; plan surface #fff.
- Typography: self-hosted, unchanged Adobe Source Sans 3, 400/600. Numeric labels
  use tabular figures. Body 14–16px; programme labels 16px/600; section labels
  18px/600; page heading 24–32px/600; numbers 64px with 80px leading target.
- Spacing: 4px base. Control padding 8/12px; card padding 16px desktop, 12px phone;
  card gaps 8px; group separation 16px; board padding 24px desktop, 16px phone.
- Depth: quiet surface shifts; no card shadows or thick decorative borders.
  Radius 4px for cells/controls and 6px for the opening surface.
- Controls: native buttons, selects and details. Source actions at least 44px.
  Filter disclosure sits before figures; active filters and Clear stay visible.
- Layout: desktop four-column grid, portrait spans the opening two rows/two
  columns, first target spans the other two columns; the support pair aligns below.
  Subsequent figures span the whole board. Below 1050px, support cells stay
  beside the portrait; on phones the portrait and quote precede the figures. At phone widths, supporting metrics become compact rows
  (number/unit beside story/source); the leading target remains prominent.
- Motion: one decorative lead sparkle, finite four-second opacity glint; disabled
  for reduced motion. Forced colours retain meaningful labels and controls.
