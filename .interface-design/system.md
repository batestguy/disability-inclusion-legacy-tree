# Inclusion scorecard

27 October 2026. Owner-approved intent: large, meaningful numbers, a short story
label and one Source link. White opening board; planned targets first, achievements
second. No repeated badges or process commentary. Preserve English/Hausa shared
vertical language control, compact bottom chatbot and existing photo-arrow cursor.

## Audience and domain

A member of the public quickly exploring Mohammed Abba Isa's disability-inclusion
work and opening a source when interested. Domain vocabulary: inclusion,
employment, enterprise, training, women, food support and public programmes.

## Direction and reference

Compact scorecard; hierarchy from number weight, programme labels and grouped
spacing. Geckoboard's charity number hierarchy plus Tableau's white data surface;
actual references are in docs/DASHBOARD_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md. The wide Beyond
Disability target and aligned supporting targets precede eight specific programme
figures. Keep all original bounds/units; never manufacture a total or progress bar.

## System

- Colours: white #fff; achievement green #156146; planned gold #83601d; ink
  #183e2e; muted #52665b; achievement surface #f4f8f5; plan surface #fcf8ed.
- Typography: self-hosted, unchanged Adobe Source Sans 3, 400/600. Numeric labels
  use tabular figures. Body 14–16px; programme labels 16px/600; section labels
  18px/600; page heading 24–32px/600; numbers 64px with 80px leading target.
- Spacing: 4px base. Control padding 8/12px; card padding 16px desktop, 12px phone;
  card gaps 8px; group separation 16px; board padding 24px desktop, 16px phone.
- Depth: quiet surface shifts; no card shadows or thick decorative borders.
  Radius 4px for cells/controls and 6px for the opening surface.
- Controls: native buttons, selects and details. Source actions at least 44px.
  Filter disclosure sits before figures; active filters and Clear stay visible.
- Layout: desktop four-column grid, first target spans two; target units align
  on a shared baseline. At phone widths, supporting metrics become compact rows
  (number/unit beside story/source); the leading target remains prominent.
- Motion: one decorative lead sparkle, finite four-second opacity glint; disabled
  for reduced motion. Forced colours retain meaningful labels and controls.
