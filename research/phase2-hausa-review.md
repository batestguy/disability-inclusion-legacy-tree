## Method explanations

| Key | English | Hausa draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scope | This dataset covers selected, checked reports. It is not a complete record of lifetime work. | Wannan kundin ya ƙunshi zaɓaɓɓun rahotannin da aka bincika. Ba cikakken tarihin dukkan ayyukan rayuwa ba ne. |
| language | The topic model analysed English articles. Hausa labels and explanations are draft translations awaiting human review. | An nazarci labaran Turanci wajen gano jigogi. Sunayen jigogi da bayanan Hausa fassarar gwaji ne da ke jiran bitar mutum. |
| topics | Themes describe patterns in published words. Filters select from one saved model; they do not retrain it. | Jigogi suna bayyana tsarin kalmomin da aka wallafa. Matatun bincike suna zaɓa daga samfurin da aka adana; ba sa sake koyar da shi. |
| connections | An article connection means two names appear in the same report. | categories | A report can have several categories. Period counts use the displayed number of selected reports as their denominator. | Rahoto na iya shiga rukuni fiye da ɗaya. Lissafin kowane lokaci ya dogara da adadin rahotannin da aka zaɓa da aka nuna. |
| figures | Delivery figures are attributed reports. Targets remain plans.
